Every command comes with a chord, and every chord can be moved.

- **Settings → Keyboard** lists every command under its menu with the chord it is on.
- **Click one and press the new chord.** A chord another command has asks you to press it twice, then moves it. `Backspace` leaves a command with none, and **Reset** puts it back; **Reset All Shortcuts** puts every one back.
- **Commands with no chord** can be given one.
- **Applied at once** to the menus, the palette, the toolbar's tooltips and the view strip.
- **Synced** with the other settings, as `keys.<command>=Shortcut+Shift+K` in the settings file, with `Shortcut` meaning `Cmd` on a Mac and `Ctrl` elsewhere.

The palette's own chord (`Alt+X` by default) moved here from General; a `Ctrl+Shift+P` chosen there is kept. Select All (`Ctrl+A`) is the list's own and stays where it is. The [Keybindings](/keybindings) page lists the defaults.
