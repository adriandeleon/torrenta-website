The sidebar keeps one choice per section, and the list shows the torrents that pass all of them, and the omnibox's filter too.

- **Status**: Downloading, Seeding, Completed, Paused, Active, Inactive, Stalled, Checking, Errored.
- **Categories** and **Tags**, including Uncategorised and Untagged.
- **Trackers**, by host, plus Trackerless and Tracker error.

Every entry has a count. `Ctrl+B` focuses the sidebar: the arrows walk every section, `Space` makes an entry its section's choice, `Enter` chooses and returns to the list, `Esc` returns without choosing, `Left` and `Right` fold a section. The palette has "Show: Downloading" and the rest, so a status is picked without leaving the list.

The sidebar sits on the left or the right, or is hidden (`Ctrl+Shift+B`); the choices, the folded sections and the place are remembered.
